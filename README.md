### 💡 What I Am Studying
* **AI Agents vs. Normal Chatbots:** Understanding how AI can take real actions instead of just typing answers.
* **Smart Failure Recovery:** Finding ways to stop AI agents from getting stuck in loops when making mistakes.
* **Running AI Locally:** Testing how lightweight AI models can run safely on normal laptops without expensive cloud servers.

---

### 📖 Key Research Paper: WebArena
* **Main Finding:** Current AI agents only succeed at ~14% of complex web tasks, while humans solve ~78%.
* **My Takeaway:** AI needs better step-by-step checking and error correction to bridge this gap.
